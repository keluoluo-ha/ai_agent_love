Shanghai Jing'an District Romantic Dating Plan

Morning (10:00-12:00): Coffee & Historic Exploration
- Start your date at PS.Café (recommended for dating with chic ambiance and greenery)
- Enjoy specialty coffee and pastries in a relaxed, elegant atmosphere
- Walk to nearby Jing'an Temple (just a 5-minute stroll) - one of Shanghai's oldest and most beautiful Buddhist temples
- Explore the temple grounds and surrounding historic architecture

Afternoon (12:30-15:30): Park Stroll & Cultural Immersion
- Visit Jing'an Park (adjacent to Jing'an Temple) - a peaceful oasis in the heart of the city
- Enjoy the beautifully landscaped gardens, lakes, and walking paths
- Find a quiet bench for conversation or rent paddle boats on the lake
- Explore the surrounding area with boutique shops and art galleries

Evening (18:30-21:00): Romantic Dinner
- Dinner at La Creperie (Jing'an) - highly-rated French restaurant (4.4/5 from 235 reviews)
- Located at Tower D, 1/F, Reel Mall, No.1601 Nanjing West Road
- Perfect for intimate conversation with authentic French cuisine and charming ambiance
- Open until 11:30 PM, so you can enjoy a leisurely meal

Night (21:30-23:00): Evening Drinks & Nightlife
- Continue the evening at one of Nanjing West Road's stylish bars:
  * Sin Lounge - sophisticated atmosphere with creative cocktails
  * Frangipani Bar - tropical-inspired cocktails in an elegant setting
  * Barbarossa Bar - classic cocktails with a modern twist
- Enjoy the vibrant yet refined nightlife of Jing'an District

Additional Romantic Touches:
- Consider visiting Prada Rong Zhai (Italian restaurant in historic building) for a special occasion
- Take a sunset walk along Nanjing West Road to see the city lights come alive
- End the evening with a traditional Shanghai dessert at a local bakery

Transportation Tips:
- Jing'an Temple has its own subway station (Lines 2, 7, 14)
- All recommended locations are within easy walking distance of each other
- Taxis and Didi (Chinese Uber) are readily available

Why This Plan Works:
✓ All locations are within 5km of Jing'an District center
✓ Balanced mix of culture, nature, food, and nightlife
✓ Progressive pace allowing for natural conversation flow
✓ Mix of traditional Shanghai charm and modern sophistication
✓ Flexible timing to accommodate your preferences
