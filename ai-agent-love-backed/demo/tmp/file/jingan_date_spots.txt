Top 5 Romantic Date Spots Within 5 km of Jing'an Temple, Shanghai

1. Jing'an Temple & Sculpture Park (0.3 km)
   - A serene Buddhist temple dating back to 247 AD, beautifully illuminated at night.
   - Adjacent Jing'an Sculpture Park offers quiet tree-lined paths, modern art, and peaceful benches — perfect for conversation and photos.
   - Ideal time: Late afternoon to sunset; avoid weekday rush hours.

2. M50 Creative Park (1.8 km)
   - Historic textile factory turned vibrant art district: galleries, indie cafés (e.g., Café Kitsuné M50), street murals, and Suzhou Creek views.
   - Laid-back, creative, and photogenic — great for couples who enjoy culture and casual strolls.
   - Bonus: Many studios host weekend artist talks or pop-up events.

3. The Bund Riverside Promenade (1.5 km)
   - Walk east from Jing'an Temple (15–20 min) or take Line 2 → East Nanjing Road (2 stops).
   - World-famous skyline views: colonial architecture by day, dazzling lights by night — iconic and effortlessly romantic.
   - Tip: Grab matcha buns from Nanxiang Steamed Bun Restaurant (near Yuyuan) before your walk.

4. Jing'an Joy City (0.5 km)
   - Upscale lifestyle mall beside Jing'an Temple Metro (Exit 13/14).
   - Rooftop garden (Joy Garden), cozy third-wave cafés (e.g., Seesaw Coffee), and frequent cultural pop-ups.
   - Great for rainy days or when you want food + shopping + ambiance in one place.

5. Sir Elly's at The Peninsula Shanghai (1.2 km)
   - Elegant fine-dining on the 13th floor with panoramic Huangpu River & Pudong views.
   - Intimate, candlelit, and service-oriented — ideal for milestone dates or special occasions.
   - Reservations essential; dress code applies. Call 021-2327-6756.

All locations are easily accessible by foot, metro (Line 2/14), or <5-min Didi ride.
